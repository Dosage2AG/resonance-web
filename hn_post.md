# Show HN: The Resonance Web -- a living network engine where death strengthens the web

Live demo: https://dosage2ag.github.io/resonance-web/demo.html
GitHub: https://github.com/Dosage2AG/resonance-web

I built a JavaScript engine for simulating emergent networks where every node is simultaneously a butterfly (sending ripples through actions) and a spider (weaving structural bonds). Nothing is known until observed. Relationships form through proximity and shared experience. When a node dies, its accumulated resonance feeds the collective, and its strongest memory becomes a permanent mark on the world. If every node carrying a piece of knowledge is removed, that knowledge is gone forever.

This came out of building ANIMA, a god-game where you guide civilizations as a formless divine presence. I needed AI creatures that actually earned their knowledge through lived experience rather than spawning pre-configured. The web that emerged turned out to be useful far beyond games -- anywhere you need to model how information propagates through relationships, decays when neglected, and leaves scars when connections break. Think organizational memory, social simulation, or earned-knowledge frameworks for AI agents.

The engine is ~270 lines, zero dependencies, MIT licensed. The demo spawns clustered creatures from a deterministic seed, lets you click to send warmth or right-click to send shadow, and you can watch the ripples propagate through bonds. The hidden accumulator tracks total resonance from every death -- it's never displayed, but it's always there.

Built solo in Madrid. Feedback welcome.
