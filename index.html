The Idea

Not a social graph. A living nervous system.

Nothing is known until observed

Nodes earn knowledge through proximity and interaction, not configuration. You don't tell the web what matters -- it discovers it.

Relationships are memory

Bonds form from shared experience and fade with distance. When a bond breaks, both sides feel it. The stronger the connection, the deeper the grief.

Actions ripple through the web

Every event propagates outward, decaying with distance and amplifying through strong bonds. Warmth heals, shadow scatters. Ripples travel up to four hops deep.

Death strengthens the web

When a node is removed, its accumulated resonance feeds the collective. Its strongest memory becomes a permanent mark on the world. A hidden accumulator tracks every death -- it is never displayed, but it is always there.

Knowledge can be permanently lost

If every node carrying a piece of knowledge is removed, that knowledge is gone forever. There is no backup. The web is mortal.

Drop it in
